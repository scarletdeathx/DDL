This license incorporates DDL-Core-2, DDL-D-2, and DDL-X-2 as allocated below.

DDL-DS-2 is a split-scope license for releases containing protected source
content and openly reusable scaffolding.

> The scaffolding is free. The content is protected.

## 1. Required Scope Designation

The release must include a clear Scope Notice that identifies its Protected
Content and Scaffold Materials by stable file path, component, field, content
address, or other reproducible boundary.

Unless the Scope Notice expressly designates material as Scaffold Materials or
identifies it as third-party or public-domain material, every part of the
release is Protected Content.

A downstream recipient may clarify the boundary for its own new contributions
but may not reclassify Protected Content as Scaffold Materials.

## 2. Protected Content

Protected Content is governed by DDL-D-2. It may be accessed, privately
researched, and evaluated as DDL-D-2 permits, but it may not be commercially
used, publicly deployed, redistributed, reconstructed, or incorporated into a
public or commercial derivative without separate written permission.

Scaffold permissions do not extend to Protected Content merely because the
content is named, indexed, described, aligned, referenced, loaded, or packaged
by the Scaffold Materials.

Recordings, source datasets, speaker-sensitive features, reconstructive
embeddings, protected annotations, and model weights are Protected Content
unless the Scope Notice expressly and lawfully states otherwise.

## 3. Scaffold Materials

Scaffold Materials are governed by DDL-X-2. They may be used, copied,
modified, distributed, publicly deployed, and commercialized, and their
Covered Utility Derivatives must remain available under DDL-X-2.

A Scaffold-only derivative should use the identifier `DDL-X-2`. A derivative
bundle that retains both Protected Content and Scaffold Materials must preserve
the `DDL-DS-2` identifier and Scope Notice.

## 4. Public-Domain and Third-Party Material

DDL-DS-2 does not claim ownership of public-domain text, facts, measurements,
or other material in which the License Holder controls no exclusive rights.
The Scope Notice must identify known public-domain or separately licensed
components when their presence could otherwise cause confusion.

A schema, selection, arrangement, annotation, or implementation may be
protected and licensed even when the facts or public-domain text represented
through it are not. DDL applies only to rights the License Holder controls.

## 5. Separation and Anti-Bleed

Separating Scaffold Materials from a DDL-DS release does not carry Protected
Content into DDL-X. Separating Protected Content does not remove its DDL-D
controls.

Generated Content produced through ordinary use of Scaffold Materials does not
automatically become Scaffold Materials or inherit DDL-X. Material produced
from Protected Content remains subject to any DDL-D restriction implicated by
its reproduction, extraction, reconstruction, training, or use.

## 6. Conflicts

For classification and scope, this DDL-DS-2 text and the release's Scope Notice
control. Within the protected zone, DDL-D-2 controls. Within the scaffold zone,
DDL-X-2 controls. DDL-Core-2 applies to both.
