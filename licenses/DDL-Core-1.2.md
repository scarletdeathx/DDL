## 1. License Family

The DDL 1.2 family consists of these operative licenses:

- DDL-D-1.2, for protected source materials and Canon Datasets.
- DDL-V-1.2, for Canon-origin models whose downstream derivatives remain
  Non-Canon unless elevated by the License Holder.
- DDL-X-1.2, for community releases and downstream lineage that must remain
  under DDL-X.

Combined identifiers such as DDL-VX-1.2 are registered profiles. A profile is
not a separate operative license unless the registry says so. A profile states
which operative license applies and what release posture the identifier is
meant to communicate.
