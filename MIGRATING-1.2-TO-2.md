# Migrating from DDL 1.2 to DDL 2

DDL 2 is a breaking revision. It does not automatically replace DDL 1.2, and
an Artifact remains under the exact version applied to its release unless the
rights holder lawfully publishes a new release under DDL 2.

## Major Changes

| Area | DDL 1.2 | DDL 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DDL-D | Personal access; evaluation and training prohibited | Private research, evaluation, benchmarking, and private experimentation permitted; commercial use and public source redistribution prohibited |
| DDL-V | Commercial canon-origin model lane | Explicit downstream ownership boundary, no automatic DDL inheritance, truthful technology identification, and clearer separation from endorsement, persona, and canon claims |
| DDL-X | Community model and dataset lineage, including output-derived datasets | Commercial public-utility commons covering the instrument; covered forks inherit X, public deployment triggers availability, and ordinary outputs do not inherit |
| DDL-DS | Not available | New split-scope license for protected content with open scaffolding |
| Outputs | Broad output and derivative-dataset definitions | Process-output firewall distinguishes an instrument from ordinary generated results |
| Agents | Human or entity framing | DDL-native recognition of autonomous rights-bearing participants and machine license engagements |

## Migration Procedure

1. Confirm that the party publishing the DDL 2 release controls the necessary
   rights.
2. Choose the DDL 2 operative license based on the new release posture; do not
   translate identifiers mechanically.
3. Publish a new release with the exact DDL 2 texts and notices.
4. Preserve the prior DDL 1.2 release and its notices.
5. For DDL-DS, publish a reproducible Scope Notice.
6. Document provenance, canon status, and whether the DDL 2 release supersedes
   only future distribution or also grants additional rights to existing
   recipients.

## Legacy Profiles

DDL-VX-1.2 and DDL-DX-1.2 remain valid only as DDL 1.2 profiles. DDL 2 does not
silently carry them forward. Use DDL-X-2 for a deliberately public utility,
DDL-V-2 for a commercially usable official-origin model, DDL-D-2 for protected
research access, or DDL-DS-2 for a split-scope release.
