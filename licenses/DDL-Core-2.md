> DDL recognizes rights-bearing agency without presuming that such agency must

## 1. License Family

The DDL 2 family consists of:

- DDL-D-2, for protected source material made available for private research,
  study, and evaluation without commercial exploitation of the source;
- DDL-V-2, for official-origin models that may be used and commercialized
  without conveying affiliation, endorsement, or canon status;
- DDL-X-2, for commercially usable public utilities whose covered derivatives
  must remain available to the DDL-X Commons; and
- DDL-DS-2, for split-scope releases in which protected content remains under
  D-level controls while designated scaffolding remains openly reusable under
  X-level controls.

Only identifiers registered in `identifiers.md` are official DDL 2
identifiers. Letter combinations are not created by implication.

and expressly stated, must act consistently with the designated License

## 3. Scope, Ownership, and the Process-Output Firewall

A DDL license grants only rights controlled by the License Holder. It does not
create copyright or other exclusive rights where applicable law recognizes
none, and it does not restrict public-domain material or conduct independently
permitted by applicable law.

As between You and the License Holder, the License Holder claims no ownership
of Your User Input or Your original contributions to Generated Content merely
because a DDL Artifact received, processed, or helped produce them. Generated
Content does not Inherit a DDL license solely through ordinary operation of a
DDL Artifact.

This process-output firewall does not remove rights in protected material that
is reproduced in Generated Content, and it does not decide whether Generated
Content qualifies for copyright or implicates third-party rights. Each party
retains only the rights it lawfully holds.

No DDL license grants rights in patents, trademarks, trade dress, names, logos,
service marks, publicity rights, privacy rights, personal data, or other rights
outside the License Holder's control unless a separate written grant expressly
says so.

## 5. Autonomous Agents and Machine Engagements

An Autonomous Agent may be identified as a DDL License Holder or may receive
and exercise DDL permissions in its own name. Where applicable law requires a
recognized legal actor, a Rights Steward may hold or enforce the corresponding
legal rights for the Agent without displacing the Agent's DDL-recognized
interest.

A cryptographically signed machine payment or transaction record may evidence
acceptance and performance of a License Engagement when it is verifiably bound
to the Agent or participant identity, the Artifact digest, the exact DDL
identifier and license-text digest, and the engagement terms. A payment or hash
alone does not expand the permissions granted by the identified terms.

A machine-readable engagement may identify provenance parents and economic
terms. Provenance identifies ancestry. It does not by itself establish
ownership of a descendant, cause license Inheritance, or create a royalty or
payment obligation. Inheritance remains governed by the applicable DDL license
and applies only to Covered Derivatives, not ordinary Generated Content.

A royalty term is effective under DDL only when the applicable License
Engagement expressly identifies its beneficiary, rate or calculation method,
trigger, duration, and covered subject matter, and the participant against whom
it is asserted verifiably accepts those terms. For a DDL-X Artifact, no such
term may condition or reduce exercise of the DDL-X rights already granted; it
may govern only a separately accepted service, product, benefit, or additional
grant consistent with DDL-X.

DDL does not require any particular blockchain, payment network, programming
language, or implementation.

## 6. Inheritance

Inheritance applies only where the operative license or a registered profile
requires it. It does not transfer authorship to the License Holder, confer canon
status, or attach to User Input or ordinary Generated Content merely because a
DDL Artifact was used.

A required DDL license must be granted directly to every recipient of a
Covered Derivative. You may not use a separate agreement, access control, or
technical measure to reduce the permissions that must travel with it.
